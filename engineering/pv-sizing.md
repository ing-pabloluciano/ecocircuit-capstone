# PV Sizing

This section documents the photovoltaic panel sizing process.

## Purpose

The PV panel must be sized to generate enough energy to recharge the battery and support the estimated daily load demand.

## Main Factors

- Total daily energy consumption
- Peak sun hours
- System efficiency losses
- Battery charging requirements
- Environmental conditions

## Basic Formula

```txt
PV Power Required = Daily Energy Demand / Peak Sun Hours
```

## Efficiency Adjustment

System losses may include:

- Panel efficiency loss
- Charge controller loss
- Wiring loss
- Battery charging loss
- Temperature effects

## Final PV Selection

The final PV panel selection is documented in the Excel calculation sheet and component selection section.
