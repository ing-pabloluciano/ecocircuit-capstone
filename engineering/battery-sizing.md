# Battery Sizing

This section documents the battery sizing process for the portable PV system.

## Purpose

The battery stores energy generated by the PV panel and provides power when sunlight is not available.

## Main Factors

- Daily energy demand
- Battery voltage
- Desired autonomy
- Depth of discharge
- Efficiency losses

## Basic Formula

```txt
Battery Capacity (Ah) = Energy Required (Wh) / Battery Voltage (V)
```

## Adjusted Capacity

The battery capacity should be adjusted based on:

- Depth of discharge
- Battery chemistry
- Safety margin
- Expected operating conditions

## Final Battery Selection

The selected battery configuration is documented in the Excel calculation sheet and component selection section.
