# Safety Considerations

This section documents safety considerations for the portable PV system.

## Electrical Safety

- Use proper wire sizing.
- Include fuses or circuit protection where needed.
- Avoid exposed conductors.
- Protect against short circuits.
- Use components within rated voltage and current limits.

## Battery Safety

- Use the correct charge controller.
- Avoid overcharging and deep discharging.
- Follow manufacturer guidelines.
- Provide ventilation if required.
- Secure the battery during transport.

## General Safety

- Keep the system protected from water exposure.
- Avoid overloading the inverter.
- Inspect wiring and connectors regularly.
- Label system components clearly.
