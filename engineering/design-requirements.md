# Design Requirements

## Objective

Design a portable photovoltaic system capable of providing electrical power for selected small loads while maintaining portability, safety, and energy efficiency.

## Functional Requirements

- Generate power using photovoltaic panels.
- Store energy using a battery system.
- Convert stored DC power into usable AC power when required.
- Support selected electrical loads.
- Remain portable and practical for field use.
- Provide basic protection and safe operation.

## Engineering Requirements

- Estimate total load demand.
- Calculate daily energy consumption.
- Size the photovoltaic panel based on expected energy needs.
- Select battery capacity based on autonomy requirements.
- Select inverter capacity based on load power.
- Consider wiring, protection, and safety factors.

## Constraints

- Portable system size
- Component availability
- Cost
- Weight
- Battery capacity
- Solar charging conditions
- Safety requirements
