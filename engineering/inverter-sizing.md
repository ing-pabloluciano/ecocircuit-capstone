# Inverter Sizing

This section documents the inverter sizing process for the portable PV system.

## Purpose

The inverter converts DC power from the battery into AC power for compatible loads.

## Main Factors

- Total AC load power
- Starting/surge power
- Continuous power rating
- System voltage
- Efficiency losses

## Basic Criteria

The inverter should be selected based on:

- Continuous wattage rating
- Surge wattage rating
- Battery voltage compatibility
- Output voltage requirements
- Safety margin

## Final Inverter Selection

The selected inverter is documented in the Excel calculation sheet and component selection section.
